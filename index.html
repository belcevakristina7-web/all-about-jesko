ALL ABOUT JESKO

The Koenigsegg Jesko is a Swedish high-performance hypercar created by Koenigsegg Automotive AB. It was unveiled at the 2019 Geneva International Motor Show and was named after Jesko von Koenigsegg, the father of company founder Christian von Koenigsegg.

The Jesko was developed with extreme performance in mind. Koenigsegg created two main versions: the track-focused Jesko Attack and the high-speed Jesko Absolut.

🔥 THE ENGINE

The Jesko uses a 5.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 engine. It was engineered by Koenigsegg to produce enormous power while remaining suitable for extreme performance.

Flat-plane crankshaft

The V8 uses a flat-plane crankshaft. According to Koenigsegg, the crankshaft weighs only about 12.5 kg and contributes to the engine's ability to rev very quickly.

Two fuel ratings

The engine is rated at up to 1,280 hp on regular gasoline and up to 1,600 hp when running on E85 fuel.

Turbo technology

The twin-turbo system is supported by an innovative air-injection system designed to reduce turbo lag and help the engine respond quickly.

⚙️ LIGHT SPEED TRANSMISSION

Instead of using a conventional dual-clutch gearbox, Koenigsegg developed its own 9-speed transmission called the Light Speed Transmission, or LST.

The system uses multiple clutches and can select different gears extremely quickly. Its name refers to the very short shift times and the ability to move between gears without following a simple sequential order.

Why is it called Light Speed?

The name is a reference to the transmission's extremely rapid gear changes rather than literally meaning the speed of light.

🪽 AERODYNAMICS

Aerodynamics are one of the most important parts of the Jesko. The body was designed to control airflow and generate significant aerodynamic downforce.

Jesko Attack

The Attack has large aerodynamic components designed for maximum cornering performance and high downforce on a circuit.

Jesko Absolut

The Absolut has a very different aerodynamic philosophy. It reduces drag and uses a much smoother body design because it is intended for very high-speed driving.

🎨 DESIGN

The Jesko combines aggressive aerodynamic shapes with Koenigsegg's lightweight engineering philosophy. Its body contains numerous aerodynamic elements that help control airflow around the car.

Rear wing

The Jesko Attack features a large active rear wing that contributes to aerodynamic performance on a race track.

Wheels

Koenigsegg developed lightweight carbon-fiber wheels for the Jesko. Reducing unsprung mass can help the car's handling and responsiveness.
